About Innovation Communications

Combining technical services and digital innovation to support business operations in Papua New Guinea.

Who We Are

A Practical Approach to Engineering and Technology

IComm, short for Innovation Communications, is a business concept focused on telecommunications-related services, engineering support, digital systems and operational reporting.

Our aim is to help businesses address everyday operational challenges through appropriate technical support, better information management and dependable service delivery.

Based in Lae, Morobe Province, IComm intends to serve businesses and organisations in Papua New Guinea, subject to project scope, capability and availability.

Our Direction

Innovation With Purpose

We believe technology should solve a real problem, make work easier and support better decisions.

  • Practical engineering solutions
  • Useful digital tools
  • Responsive customer service
  • Responsible business growth

Our Mission

To provide practical technical and digital solutions that help customers improve operational efficiency and manage information.

Our Vision

To develop into a trusted Papua New Guinean provider of engineering support and business technology solutions.

Our Values

Integrity, accountability, customer focus, safety and continuous improvement guide our intended service standards.

Our Approach
